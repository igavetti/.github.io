About Me

I am a PhD student in Neuroendocrinology at the University of Lille, working in the Lille Neuroendocrinology group under the supervision of Dr. Konstantina Chachlaki.

My academic background combines cognitive science, artificial intelligence, and computational neuroscience. I am particularly interested in how inflammatory processes following preterm birth interact with neuroendocrine functioning, and how computational approaches can help model these mechanisms.
