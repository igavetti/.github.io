About Me

I am a PhD student in Neuroendocrinology at the University of Lille, working under the supervision of Dr. Konstantina Chachlaki.

My academic background bridges cognitive science, artificial intelligence, and computational neuroscience. I am interested in how inflammatory processes following preterm birth interact with neuroendocrine systems, and how computational models can help disentangle these mechanisms.
